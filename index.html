Biography & Heritage

The Leadership Journey of Zeenat Kureshi

A distinguished career dedicated to international diplomacy, cultural storytelling, and national community stewardship.

A Catalyst for Global Transformation

Zeenat Kureshi stands at the intersection of diplomacy, film production, and nationwide civic leadership. As the GCC–India Trade Commissioner, she spearheads institutional linkages that accelerate bilateral investments and cultural exchange between the Gulf Cooperation Council nations and India.

In the entertainment industry, she has produced transformative cinematic projects that bridge regional narratives with universal appeal. Simultaneously, her tenure as the National President of the All India Jamiatul Quresh Women Cell has established enduring welfare and entrepreneurial programs for over 50,000 women nationwide.
